Privacy — MyDoas

MyDoas stores your saved Doas, repetition counts, and display preferences on your device.

When you choose Use my location, the app requests your device’s approximate location from your browser and sends the coordinates to the AlAdhan prayer-times service to calculate the day’s Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha times. The app does not store your location. If browser location permission is unavailable, the app may request an approximate location from a third party to provide prayer times. The approximate location is not stored by the app.

Payment is handled on the payment page shown by the app. The current transaction-reference entry is a self-reported claim and is not independently verified by the app.

For questions, contact the app publisher through the channel where you received this app.
